## Patterns
1. Winners are single-file, zero-dependency, interactive: math/sim + generative audio + a point of view.
2. Models converge on the same genre (consciousness meditation + fractals), so novelty is the differentiator.
3. Long autonomous runs (hours) that end in a watchable artifact get shared most.
4. The backlash is over-building when the user wanted control.
