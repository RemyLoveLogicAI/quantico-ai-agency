# POOR INSIGHT
*An adult animated limited series · 6 × 22 min · TV-MA*
**Pilot: "Ideas of Reference"**

---

## THE PITCH

**Logline.** Gideon Pike has schizophrenia, a seven-day pill organizer, and a binder of 1,204 numbered observations that he is being followed. Both things are true: the diagnosis *and* the conspiracy. The man running it is the one person in Port Mercy that everybody believes.

**Tone.** BoJack's grief, Archer's cruelty, Mr. Robot's dread, shot in the break room of a company that sells it all as *wellness*.

### The five rules of the show

1. **Gideon is sick. Gideon is also right. Neither cancels the other.** Medication quiets his voices. It does nothing for his landlord.
2. **The conspiracy is boring.** No lizard men, no black helicopters. A rich man, a calendar, and a great many favors. Evil is mostly scheduling.
3. **The stalkers are gig workers.** *Bystandr: Be Present. Get Paid.* Eleven dollars an hour, rated out of five. Half of them think it's immersive theatre.
4. **The Stamp.** A rubber stamp slams into the corner of the frame for the audience only: `STATUS: HAPPENING`, `STATUS: HEAD`, `STATUS: UNCONFIRMED`. Gideon never sees it, which is the point: he has to live without the stamp. Once a season, the Stamp lies, and prints a correction.
5. **Never mock the illness. Mock everyone who profits from it.**

### Look & sound

- **Two frame rates, one world.** The city is flat, municipal grey vector animation. Gideon's perception bleeds over it as hand-scratched graphite marginalia: arrows, tallies, numbered circles. Over the season the arrows turn out to be accurate. The Bystandr ops floor is saturated, bright, sitcom-warm: the horror is that it's a workplace comedy.
- **The Committee.** Gideon's three voices are a plummy nature-documentary narrator (**MR. PELL**), a gravel-voiced chain-smoker (**DOT**), and a terrified new hire who keeps asking if he should be taking notes (**THE INTERN**). They are *the illness, not the conspiracy*, and they are wrong at least as often as they're right. They frequently side with the doctor.
- **The chime.** The Lantern jingle: three soft notes. It's in elevators, hold music, ads, the pharmacy. By the end of the pilot it's in his mouth.

### Season map: every title is a clinical term that turns out to describe the villains

1. **Ideas of Reference**: everything *is* about him.
2. **Thought Insertion**: someone is feeding him ideas, and this time it isn't the Committee.
3. **Flat Affect**: Priya quits. Terry's dental plan doesn't.
4. **Command Hallucination**: the voice at 3 a.m. isn't his. The smoke detectors talk back.
5. **Anosognosia**: Gideon stops his medication as a tactic, and it works exactly as well as you'd fear.
6. **Insight (Poor)**: the evidence arrives. The question is whether being believed was ever the point.

# PILOT: "IDEAS OF REFERENCE"

**COLD OPEN**

**INT. BUS 14: MORNING**

*Flat municipal grey. GIDEON PIKE, 34, ironed shirt, a lanyard from a job that no longer exists, thumbs open the Tuesday compartment of a pill organizer. Handwritten marginalia scratch themselves across the frame as he counts the passengers.*

**GIDEON (V.O.)**
Eleven passengers. Four are behaving normally. Seven are doing something with their faces.

*A WOMAN across the aisle unwraps a granola bar. Crinkle. Crinkle. A long crinkle.*

**MR. PELL (V.O.)**
*(plummy, hushed)*
And here, the commuter forages.

**DOT (V.O.)**
It's a granola bar, Gideon.

**THE INTERN (V.O.)**
*(whispering)*
Should I be writing this down?

**GIDEON**
*(barely audible)*
Short, short, long. That's not a granola bar. That's Morse.

*A red STAMP slams into the corner of the frame:*

> **STATUS: HEAD**

*Behind Gideon, a MAN lowers a physical newspaper, the way people only do in movies about spies, and photographs the back of Gideon's head with a flip phone.*

> **STATUS: HAPPENING**

*Gideon doesn't see either stamp. He rubs the back of his neck. Frowns. Rubs it again.*

**DOT (V.O.)**
...Don't turn around.

*SMASH CUT TO a text thread on the flip phone. BIG TERRY thumbs it out, two fingers, painfully:*

**TERRY (V.O.)**
"Subj exited bus 8:04. Took pills. Quick question is bus fare reimbursed or"

**MAIN TITLE: POOR INSIGHT.** *The three-note Lantern chime.*

---

**ACT ONE**

**INT. LANTERN WELLNESS COLLECTIVE: WAITING ROOM: DAY**

*Beige, tasteful, designed to be forgiven. Poster: YOUR MIND IS A GARDEN over a stock photo of a hand cupping a seedling. Another: YOU ARE NOT ALONE, with a little lamp logo. The sign-in clipboard already has GIDEON PIKE printed at the top of the sheet.*

**RECEPTIONIST**
Morning, Gideon! Dr. Osei-Banks is ready for you. And Lowell wanted me to tell you he's thinking of you.

**GIDEON**
He says that every week.

**RECEPTIONIST**
Isn't it nice that he thinks of you every week?

**GIDEON**
It's something.

**INT. DR. OSEI-BANKS'S OFFICE: CONTINUOUS**

*DR. NNEKA OSEI-BANKS, 45, a good doctor in a bad system, a mug that reads WORLD'S OKAYEST PSYCHIATRIST. Gideon sets a three-inch binder on her desk like a body.*

**GIDEON**
The Ledger. Entries 1,189 through 1,204.

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
How many have you verified?

**GIDEON**
Nineteen. Lifetime.

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
And of the nineteen?

**GIDEON**
Eleven were nothing. A man was just tying his shoe.

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
And the other eight?

**GIDEON**
...Unfalsifiable.

*She takes off her glasses.*

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
Gideon. The term for what you describe—

**GIDEON**
"Ideas of reference." I know. A license plate, a billboard, a granola bar, and my brain says *that's about me.*

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
And?

**GIDEON**
And the brain's right sometimes. That's what nobody puts on the pamphlet.

*Long beat. She chooses her words like someone crossing ice.*

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
I'm going to say something, and I need you not to hear it as a win. Some of it may be real. And you may still be unwell. Both.

**GIDEON**
Do you know that's the nicest and worst thing anyone's ever said to me?

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
The hard part of your illness was never that you see patterns, Gideon. It's that some of them are there.

*Her phone buzzes on the desk, face up. A preview slides across it:*

> **LOWELL: How's G doing? Soup's on me 🍲 xx**

*She reads it. Something crosses her face. She turns the phone face down.*

**GIDEON**
Who's that?

**DR. OSEI-BANKS**
Pharmacy.

> **STATUS: HAPPENING** *(she is a good doctor and a terrible liar)*

---

**INT. BYSTANDR LOCAL OPS HUB: STRIP MALL: DAY**

*Wedged between a vape shop and a tax preparer. Everything is the color of a Saturday-morning cartoon, a different frame rate from Gideon's world: warm, jittery, buzzing. A whiteboard: THIS WEEK'S WIN: FEWER THAN 3 "HI!"s. A poster: ALL BYSTANDERS ARE ESSENTIAL.*

*ODALYS BRANDT, 58, headset, cardigan, a lanyard of eleven keys, addresses two new hires: PRIYA, 26, in a thrift-store trench coat, and BIG TERRY, 52, still carrying yesterday's newspaper.*

**ODALYS**
Welcome to Bystandr. You are not employees. You are *Ambient Presences.* Eleven dollars an hour, plus tips.

**PRIYA**
There are tips?

**ODALYS**
There are not tips. Your Subject is codenamed Birdwatcher.

**PRIYA**
Why Birdwatcher?

**ODALYS**
Because he watches everything, constantly. It's exhausting for the rest of us.

**PRIYA**
*(raising a hand)*
So this is, like, immersive theatre? Sleep No More, but the audience is one guy?

*Odalys looks at her for a long moment. Then, unexpectedly, a small and sincere smile.*

**ODALYS**
That is a *wonderful* way to think about it. Do not tell him it's immersive. It ruins the immersion.

**PRIYA**
What's my motivation?

**ODALYS**
To be near the pharmacy.

**PRIYA**
Why?

**ODALYS**
Because he's going to the pharmacy.

**TERRY**
*(aside, to Priya)*
Rule one: never hold eye contact more than a second and a half. I got a stopwatch in my head.

**ODALYS**
Rule two: no touching. Rule three: if he confronts you, you say, "Sorry, do I know you?" Warmly, with a small head tilt. Rule four: this is not stalking. This is proximity-based community engagement.

**TERRY**
Rule five: we're not a gang. We're a *group.* We have a Slack.

---

**INT. PHARMACY: DAY**

*Gideon in aisle six, reading a label. In the next aisle: TERRY, PRIYA, and three other BYSTANDERS each reach for the same brand of waxed floss, in perfect silence. Perfect unison.*

**THE INTERN (V.O.)**
That's a lot of floss.

**DOT (V.O.)**
Gid. That is *a lot* of floss.

**MR. PELL (V.O.)**
A rare convergence. The floss bloom.

> **STATUS: HAPPENING**

*Gideon writes in the Ledger. Terry approaches, rehearsed, too loud.*

**TERRY**
Hey! Sorry! Is this the... aisle... for... dental?

**GIDEON**
Which dental?

**TERRY**
*(sweating)*
...The waxed.

**GIDEON**
Floss doesn't actually prevent cavities. The evidence is weak. It's mostly a marketing campaign.

**TERRY**
*(to himself, genuinely)*
Huh. Oh, that's... huh.

*Gideon removes a six-sided die from his pocket and rolls it on the counter. It lands on three. He consults a hand-drawn route map.*

**GIDEON**
Three. The aquatic center.

*Behind him, a pharmacist nods. Everyone in the frame watches him go. Every one of them is acting like they weren't.*

---

**INT. OPS HUB: CONTINUOUS**

*A map on a wall screen. The blue dot is moving the wrong way. ODALYS stares at it.*

**ODALYS**
He's going where?

**BYSTANDER (O.S.)**
The municipal pool.

**ODALYS**
Who has swimwear?

*Silence. A cough somewhere.*

**TERRY**
I got a Speedo from 2009 in my glovebox.

**ODALYS**
Why.

**TERRY**
It's a long story, and the story is that I was a man with hope.

**PRIYA**
I have a mermaid tail. From Halloween.

*Odalys closes her eyes. Breathes. Opens them.*

**ODALYS**
Fine. *Fine.*

---

**INT. PORT MERCY AQUATIC CENTER: LAP POOL: DAY**

*Underwater. Muffled. The marginalia stop. The Committee goes silent for the first time in the pilot. GIDEON floats on his back, face up, in the slow lane, and breathes. For twenty seconds the show gives us his peace: no stamps, no scribbles, no chime. The animation softens into watercolor.*

*Then he surfaces.*

*Three synchronized swimmers in the next lane. A man in a Speedo and a lanyard. And, in the deep end, a woman in a full mermaid tail executing a slow, committed backstroke.*

> **STATUS: HAPPENING** *(unfortunately)*

*Gideon climbs out. Dripping. Points.*

**GIDEON**
I KNOW YOU'RE WITH THEM.

*PRIYA, mid-tail, treading water, improvising so hard her eyes water.*

**PRIYA**
...With who?

**GIDEON**
The... the—

*He can't name it. He's never had a name for it.*

**GIDEON** *(CONT'D)*
...The Arrangement.

**PRIYA**
*(warm, head tilt)*
Sorry. Do I know you?

*A lifeguard's whistle. Every swimmer in the pool turns to look at the soaking man yelling at a woman in a mermaid tail. We see him through their eyes: a sick man. A scene. A story they'll tell at dinner.*

**GIDEON**
*(small)*
...Oh.

*His marginalia scratch themselves across the frame, a loop of arrows chasing their own tail:*

*REACTION = SYMPTOM. NO REACTION = CONSENT. ←*

*He can't win by being right in public.*

**ACT TWO**

**EXT. AQUATIC CENTER: ENTRANCE: LATER**

*A squad car, lights lazy. DET. RAY KOWALCZYK, 50s, rumpled, tired, decent enough to be dangerous. OFFICER TREVOR LISKE, 24, jumpy, new. Gideon in a towel and wet jeans, backpack at his feet.*

**RAY**
Gideon. Ray. We've met.

**GIDEON**
Fourteen times.

*Ray checks a tablet. A pause.*

**RAY**
Fourteen welfare checks. Every one called in by the same concerned friend.

**GIDEON**
L. Fairweather.

**RAY**
Mm.

*Ray scratches his neck.*

**RAY** *(CONT'D)*
In twenty years I've never met a man who got fourteen welfare checks from the same guy who wasn't either the most loved or the most watched man in the city.

*Gideon, nervous, reaches into the backpack.*

**OFFICER LISKE**
HANDS! HANDS!

*Everything freezes. The frame desaturates. The sound of a heartbeat. Liske's hand on his holster. Gideon's hand frozen halfway into the bag. For a full ten seconds the show does not joke.*

**RAY**
*(very quietly)*
Easy, Trevor. Easy. Gideon, what's in the bag?

**GIDEON**
*(shaking)*
...A binder.

*He slowly pulls it out. The Ledger.*

**RAY**
That's a binder.

**GIDEON**
It's evidence.

**RAY**
*(exhaling)*
It's a binder, Trevor.

*A white sprinter van pulls up. A little lamp logo: LANTERN MOBILE CARE UNIT: YOU'RE NOT ALONE. The sliding door opens.*

*LOWELL FAIRWEATHER, 41. Quarter-zip that costs more than the car. A face that's been told it's trustworthy since age nine. A blanket already in his hands. He steps out and the palette of the whole frame warms by a degree. Every posture in the scene relaxes. This is how he does it.*

**LOWELL**
Hey. Hey, buddy. It's me.

*He drapes the blanket over Gideon like a priest.*

**LOWELL** *(CONT'D)*
Trevor, right? How's your mom's hip? And Ray, how was Dani's recital?

**RAY**
*(reluctantly)*
...It went well. Thanks, Lowell.

*Lowell turns to Gideon. The camera goes tight. The sound drops out of the entire world. We hear only Lowell's voice, and for the first time it has no warmth in it at all.*

**LOWELL** *(whispering)*
The pool. Clever. Dice? You always did like dice.

*Beat.*

**LOWELL** *(CONT'D)*
Almost.

*The sound returns. The warmth returns. Lowell cups Gideon's face with one hand, loudly, lovingly, for everyone.*

**LOWELL** *(CONT'D)*
I love you, Gid. Nobody's doing anything. Let's get you home.

**GIDEON**
You're doing this.

**LOWELL**
*(to the cops, sad, apologetic, a parent whose toddler has said something rude)*
This is the part where he thinks it's me. It's okay. It's always me. I'm the one who's close.

*Ray nods slowly. Liske nods. The lifeguard nods. From the pool deck, PRIYA, still in the mermaid tail, nods regretfully, then flops over.*

> **STATUS: HAPPENING**
> *(he is very good)*

**ACT THREE**

**INT. LANTERN HOUSE: UNIT 4C: NIGHT**

*A studio apartment the size of a regret. On the wall, not red string but a neat grid of index cards: DATE / EVENT / WITNESS / LIKELIHOOD. It's an audit, not a lunatic's wall. It looks like the work of a man who was very good at his job.*

*A Tupperware of soup sits outside his door. Handwritten label: CHICKEN & STARS. EAT. — L. Gideon doesn't touch it. He carries it to a shelf already lined with seven identical Tupperware containers, each dated, each untouched, each at a different stage of decay. A controlled experiment.*

**THE INTERN (V.O.)**
Are we not eating the soup?

**DOT (V.O.)**
We are not eating the soup.

*On the ceiling, a smoke detector. A tiny green LED blinks every four seconds. Gideon looks at it. It looks back.*

*He takes down a lease agreement, 52 pages, and opens it to page 41. Highlighted: "WELLNESS MONITORING ADDENDUM. Resident consents to ambient Safety Lantern™ sensing in all living areas for the purposes of proactive care." Signature, dated eleven days into his hospitalization. Beside the signature, in a different hand: VOLUNTARY.*

**GIDEON**
*(to no one, to the room, to us)*
That's the thing nobody understands. I'm not looking for a hidden camera. It's *disclosed.* The conspiracy is notarized.

**DOT (V.O.)**
Page forty-one of fifty-two. Nobody reads page forty-one.

**GIDEON**
I read page forty-one. I just read it from a gurney.

**MR. PELL (V.O.)**
A tragedy in three parts.

**THE INTERN (V.O.)**
Should I—

**DOT (V.O.)**
Yes. Write that down.

*Gideon dials. A school gymnasium, bake-sale chaos behind her. JO PIKE-MARSH, 37, loving, exhausted.*

**JO (V.O., PHONE)**
Gid. It's nine at night. Did you take the evening ones?

**GIDEON**
Jo. I need the Tupperware.

**JO**
The... freezer Tupperware? The one that says DO NOT EAT — EVIDENCE?

**GIDEON**
Don't tell Lowell.

**JO**
Why would I tell Lowell?

*A beat that goes on a second too long.*

**JO** *(CONT'D)*
He paid for Mom's hip, Gid. I'm just saying he's — he's *family.*

**GIDEON**
Jo.

**JO**
...I'll bring it Thursday. Okay? I love you. Take your pills.

*She hangs up. Gideon looks at the phone. A small stamp lands, almost gently:*

> **STATUS: HAPPENING**
> *(she isn't lying. She's worse: she's grateful.)*

**INT. OPS HUB: LATE NIGHT**

*Fluorescent hum. ODALYS locks a drawer, shrugs on a coat.*

**ODALYS**
Terry, lights when you're done. Priya, you're late for your nothing.

*She exits. TERRY stacks chairs. PRIYA lingers at a laptop. The tab reads SUBJECT HISTORY. Three folders: BIRDWATCHER (ACTIVE). ROBIN (CLOSED). WREN (CLOSED).*

*She clicks WREN. A photo: a woman in her sixties, a county clerk's lanyard.*

> **MARIANNE WELLS. Retired county clerk. Flagged discrepancies in Lantern Housing Trust filings. Closed: Subject relocated (county facility, long-term).**

*She clicks ROBIN.*

> **CLOSED: Subject deceased.**

*Priya's hand goes over her mouth. She scrolls to the morning's log:*

> **BUS 14 AM: T. BOUDREAUX (NEWSPAPER). M. ADEYEMI (GRANOLA: "SHORT, SHORT, LONG: ACKNOWLEDGE SUBJECT." PLAYBOOK P. 4).**

*Priya stares. Whispers it aloud.*

**PRIYA**
...The granola bar was real.

*On the wall screen behind her, a replay of the cold open: the woman crinkling the granola bar. The old stamp, HEAD, is struck through. A new one slams over it:*

> **CORRECTION: "STATUS: HEAD" (08:01, BUS 14) → "STATUS: HAPPENING."**
> **We told you it was in his head. So did everyone else.**

*Behind Priya, Terry stands in the doorway. He has seen the screen. A long beat. He could say something. He could do something.*

*He reaches for the light switch. Turns the lights off. Then, very carefully, turns them back on and walks out, leaving Priya in the glow.*

---

**INT. FAIRWEATHER RESIDENCE: KITCHEN: NIGHT**

*Gorgeous. Copper pans. A child's drawing on the fridge, crayon: MY DAD HELPS PEOPLE. LOWELL, apron on, stirs a pot of chicken and stars, humming the three-note chime to himself. On a wall-sized display above the island, a grid of tiles: UNIT 4C, from the smoke detector's point of view. Gideon sits on the bed, tiny, writing.*

*Lowell ladles soup into a Tupperware. Labels it in marker. Adds a small heart. His phone lights.*

> **JO ♥: He asked for the Tupperware.**

*Lowell types, thumb-slow, gentle.*

> **LOWELL: Thank you. Bring it to me instead. Don't worry him.**
> **JO ♥: OK 💛**

*He sets the phone down. Looks at the wall. Adjusts a slider with one finger, dimming the tile like a nightlight.*

**LOWELL**
*(softly, to the screen)*
Sleep, buddy.

**TAG**

**INT. UNIT 4C: DEEP NIGHT**

*Dark. Gideon lies awake. The green LED blinks. Four seconds. Four seconds.*

*He raises a hand and waves at it, slowly, almost tenderly.*

**GIDEON**
I know you're there.

*Silence. The LED blinks. Then, soft, from somewhere in the ceiling, three notes: the Lantern chime.*

**GIDEON** *(CONT'D)*
...Was that you?

*The LED blinks. He listens. Then he realizes his own lips are moving. He's been humming it. For how long, he doesn't know.*

**DOT (V.O.)**
Gid.

**GIDEON**
What.

**DOT (V.O.)**
That's theirs.

*He stops. Silence. The LED. The room.*

**THE INTERN (V.O.)**
*(very small)*
Should I... write that one down?

**DOT (V.O.)**
...No, kid. Not that one.

*Gideon closes the Ledger. Lies back. The Stamp, hovering at the edge of the frame, finally comes down. It takes its time.*

> **STATUS: UNCONFIRMED**

*SMASH TO BLACK. The three notes.*

**END OF PILOT.**

## WHAT COMES NEXT

**Episode 2, "Thought Insertion" (teaser).** A stranger on a bench leaves Gideon a clue. The Committee, for once, votes unanimously: *don't.* The clue is real. So is the trap.

**Production notes.**
- Staff the room with lived-experience writers and a clinical consultant. The show's credibility depends on the illness being drawn with precision and respect.
- The Stamp is a recurring *formal* device, not a gimmick: the audience's trust in it is the show's weapon, and the pilot's correction is the first time it's turned on us.
- The Bystandr ops floor should be the funniest set on TV. The harassment is the horror. The break room is the joke. Don't let them bleed.
